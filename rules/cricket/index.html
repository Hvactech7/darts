Cricket

Close 20 down to 15 and the bull, then score

2–4 players · Free · Works offline

Play Cricket free →

The idea

Only seven targets matter: 20, 19, 18, 17, 16, 15 and the bull. You need three hits to close each one, and you score points on the numbers you have closed and they have not.

Your turn

Three darts, tapped on the board (the little board in the header opens it). Each dart is worth its marks: a single = 1, a double = 2, a triple = 3. So one T20 closes the 20 outright.

Bull is the same idea: the outer bull is 1 mark, the Double Bull is 2.

The marks

One hit shows a single blade, two hits cross into an X, and the third stamps a gold seal — that number is yours.

Scoring points

Once you have closed a number, every further hit on it scores — until they close it too. You have the 20 sealed and throw T20? That is 60 points on the board.

When both sides have closed a number the row goes dead and greys out — nobody wastes another dart on it.

Winning

You win when you have closed all seven targets and you are level or ahead on points. Closed everything but behind? You have to keep scoring on whatever is still open until you catch up.

Good to know

That is the whole tactic: close what you can score on, and shut down the number they keep feeding on.
