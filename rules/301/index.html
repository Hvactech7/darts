301

Double in, double out — the short, mean one

2–4 players · Free · Works offline

Play 301 free →

The idea

Same race as 501, from 301 down to exactly zero — but you have to open with a double and finish with one.

Doubling in

Nothing counts until you hit a double. Throw 20, 20, 20 to start and you are still sitting on 301 — the app flashes a reminder and the score does not move.

The moment you hit a double it opens you and scores. D20 as your opener puts you on 261.

Your turn

Three darts, then Score It — either punch the total into the calculator or press 🎯 Dart Board and tap each dart. On the board the app knows a double when it sees one, so it opens you automatically.

Finishing

The dart that lands you exactly on zero must also be a double (thin outer ring, or the Double Bull). Past zero, or left on 1, is a bust — turn wiped, back to where you started it.

Good to know

301 is short, so the double-in is most of the game. Two players stuck outside the double can trade blanks for a while — that is normal, and the scoreboard shows a warning until you are in.
