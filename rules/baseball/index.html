Baseball

Nine innings on the 1 through 9

2–8 players · Free · Works offline

Play Baseball free →

The idea

Nine innings, and each inning has its own number: inning 1 is the 1s, inning 2 the 2s, up to the 9s. You are collecting runs.

Your turn

Three darts at the inning’s number. A single is 1 run, a double is 2, a triple is 3. Nothing else scores — a fat 20 in the 4th inning is just a miss.

Walk through one

Inning 4. You throw T4, D4 and a miss: that is 3 + 2 = 5 runs for the inning. A perfect inning is three triples for 9.

Winning

Most runs after nine innings. Level? Extra innings, up the numbers, until somebody comes out ahead.

Good to know

The small numbers are all crowded at the bottom of the board, so early innings are scrappier than they look.
