  • What the head can do. The head runs the league. They can:
    • add, remove and merge names;
    • approve moves between teams;
    • fix claims;
    • set the league's weekly night;
    • receive the night's report.
  • The league isn't one person's. It doesn't end when its creator stops playing.
    • The head role can be handed to another member.
    • If a head leaves and nobody can reach them, another member can take over the head role as the app allows.
    • If people disagree about who controls a league, we may ask for information. We then decide, acting reasonably, and our decision is final.
  • League data is for the league.
    • The roster, results, stats and members' emails are there to run the league.
    • Heads and members may not use them to sell, advertise or solicit, or share them outside the league.
    • Members' emails may be visible to the head, and the head's email may be visible to members.
  • League nights.
    • Games count toward the league when League is on for that night. A league's scheduled night turns it on and off automatically on every league device.
    • Games scored with League off are kept and can be added later by someone who chooses to.
    • The head and the league decide what counts. Vexcom doesn't.
