Privacy Policy

Dart Keeper is made by Vexcom LLC, Vacaville, California. Effective November 4, 2026. This policy covers the website at dartkeeper.net, the web app at dartkeeper.net/play and the Dart Keeper apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version

Dart Keeper keeps score. Your games live on your own phone or tablet, and each finished game is also sent to our server so leagues, standings and stats work. If you add an email address, we use it to back your players up, restore them on a new device and run your league. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads. The app never asks for your location.

What we collect

  • Players and games: the player names you type, the teams and groups you make, and every game's scores, turns and stats. All of this is stored on your device.
  • Email addresses: yours, if you enter one. It identifies your backup, confirms that a league name belongs to you and receives the messages described below. If you save an email address on a player's card, it's sent to our server too, so that player gets their league's reports.
  • Game records: when a game ends, the app sends our server a record of it:
    • the winners' and losers' names, which game it was, and each player's scoring (points, darts thrown, high turn, 180s, tons and checkouts);
    • the device's time zone and language, an anonymous device identifier created by the app, the app version, and your email address if you've entered one.
    A match played with League off is also sent whole, every leg and its scores, so a night scored with the switch forgotten is never lost. It isn't added to any league unless someone chooses to add it. Our server adds your public internet address at that moment, the city, region, country and network provider that address maps to, and a scrambled fingerprint of the address, so games played on the same wifi can be grouped together. This is how league history works, and how we learn where Dart Keeper is being used. You can turn it off with Share game records in Player Library › Backup & Share, and it stays off. With it off, finished games and the install record stay on your device, a match played with League off can't be added to a league later, and Player Card emails reach us only with your league's data. League play still syncs as described below.
  • Install: once, when the app is first installed, it tells our server that an install happened and, if you arrived through a QR code, which code.
  • Page views: our website, and the web app when you open it in a browser, count page views using Cloudflare Web Analytics. Launches from a home-screen copy or the Dart Keeper apps aren't counted, and turning off Share game records stops the counting too. It records the page, the referring site, the country and the kind of browser. It sets no cookies, stores no identifier on your device and can't recognize you on another site or on a later visit.

Who sees what

  • People in your league see the roster, the results and the stats of league play, including your name and how you did. Members can email the league head from the app.
  • The league head can remove or merge names and receives the night's report.
  • If someone else added you: league heads and teammates often type in other players' names, and sometimes their email addresses. If you'd like your name or email removed, ask your league head, or write to privacy@vexcomllc.com and we'll remove or anonymize it, even if you've never used Dart Keeper yourself.

Services we use

  • Our server keeps your backup (tied to your email) so you can restore on a new device, and keeps each league's roster, teams, results and members' email addresses. It's operated by Vexcom LLC and runs on Cloudflare in the United States.
  • Resend, an email delivery service, sends backup notices, restore codes, sign-in links, league confirmation links, league reports and boot notices. We send only the messages the app needs. You can change which league reports you get from the link at the bottom of any league email.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views as described above. It's given no name, no email and no play data.
  • GitHub Pages hosts our website and the web app. Like any web host, it sees your internet address when your browser loads a page.
  • Google Play Integrity may let the Android app check that it's a genuine, unmodified copy. Google handles the check, and it doesn't include your name, email or play data.

Fonts, images and the dartboard are built into the app. Apart from these, the app talks to no one.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade your information, or share it for advertising.
  • We don't show advertising and don't use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't ask for or collect your device's location. The city on a game record comes from the internet address, not from GPS, and is often only approximate.
  • We don't give player email addresses to advertisers or other companies, apart from the email service that delivers our messages.

How long we keep it

Your backup stays until you replace it or ask us to delete it. A league stays as long as its head keeps it. Game records are kept so league stats and history keep working. To have everything tied to your email removed from our server, follow the steps on Delete your data. Games you played in a league stay in that league's history, but we'll remove or anonymize your name in them if you ask.

California residents

You can ask us what personal information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to privacy@vexcomllc.com. We'll confirm the request comes from you before acting on it. We don't sell or share personal information. Because we don't track you across sites, the app works the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Children

Dart Keeper is for players 13 and older; players under 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. Please don't add a child under 13 to the app. If you believe a child's information is in Dart Keeper, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
